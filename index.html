September 30, 2026

An unusually busy month full of travel. I checked off four new states!

  • Over Labor Day weekend I went to Delaware for the first time. I enjoyed both the charm of Lewes ("the first city in the first state") and the beach culture of Rehoboth

  • I did a proper trip out west to see Lake Tahoe/Reno (NV), Grand Teton NP (WY), Yellowstone NP (WY/MT), and Craters of the Moon NM (ID). All beautiful in different ways, and I came out really liking Idaho

  • I've been continuing to work with Claude Code. Right now my focus is on improving my engineering standards/code review pipeline. The goal is to be able to learn to let go of the code and really embrace the project management/testing side of the development process

  • My Framework 13 laptop's battery expanded so much it unplugged the keyboard. While fun to break out the iFixit set, I would've preferred to not have to go to the fire department again

  • Took a week long trip to FL to visit my parents. It was wonderful being on the beach and we created some fun crayon art together

  • Finally checked off a long-running item from my life experiences list by riding a horse for the first time. It was fun but a lot weirder than expected

  • For the past few months I've been slowly selling off my old CD collection. The trips to the post office have finally been slowing down; I think almost everything valuable has been bought

  • A gracious friend bought me a coffee sampler from a one-man specialty roaster in NYC. It's inspired me to spice up my coffee sourcing/roasting soon
