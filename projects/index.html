Projects

On this page you'll find an assortment of what I've been working on. As you can see, it both intersects with my interests and also shows you my development approach. Everything here is on GitHub and licensed so others can use it.

Music Library Curation

For those of us who still maintain a local music library, upkeep is paramount (and part of the fun!).

Lyricsmith

An advanced suite for adding synced and untimed lyrics to all your songs. No need to be jealous of anyone with a streaming service.

  • Album

    Finds both synced and untimed lyrics for every track on an album using a variety of sources. It supports writing both sidecars and direct embeddings into MP3s.

  • Sweep

    Runs Album across a whole library, one album at a time.

  • Sync

    For songs that only have untimed lyrics available, this matches them to the song's own audio. It works offline to separate the vocals and align the words to them.

  • Transcribe

    For songs with no lyrics available (or genres like stand-up comedy that generally don't have them), this generates timed and untimed lyrics offline directly from the audio file.

Apple Music Toolbox

For the Mac users out there, a set of tools to get more out of the Apple Music desktop app.

  • Album Stats

    Reports various stats about an album such as track count, favorite tracks, and year.

  • Recently Added

    Finds the most recently added albums to Apple Music.

  • Tag Refresh

    Makes the Music app reread the tags of MP3s that changed on disk, so the lyrics, titles and album names it shows match the files again.

Development Refinements

Frictions and risks exist in every development environment and have a way of demanding a fix.

Claude Tools

Working with Claude is great, but it definitely has its quirks, especially regarding sessions and token usage. Here are a few tools to improve the experience. Note that these might go out of date one day if Anthropic gets its act together.

  • Usage Window Primer

    Keeps a Claude subscription's five-hour usage window running while the machine is unattended, so a fresh window is already open when you return.

  • Remote Session Reviver

    A Claude desktop app scheduled task that keeps the app's Code-tab sessions reachable from a phone through Remote Control, even after app updates and restarts turn it off.

APFS TM Snapfree

Git worktrees can sometimes grow out of control on your hard drive when you have a lot of sessions going on. This is one way of permanently containing them on a Mac.

Git Workspace Backup

We all know developing on a single computer can be dangerous, even with remote version control. That doesn't always stop us from doing it though... This tool keeps a daily backup of an entire environment on an external drive.

Website Flourishes

Of course, we can't leave out this website itself! This section is a collection of tools made as part of creating this site.

Decorative Crossword

A fun generator so you can go from a word bank to a puzzle. The emphasis here is on relaxing the rules just enough to focus on the design.

Media Feeds

Various tools for querying both local and network APIs to generate reports on recent media consumption history.

  • Album Scan · Cover Art

    Finds the most recently added albums and fetches each one's cover art at a chosen size.

  • Film Scan · Wikidata Director

    Reads a public Letterboxd diary feed for the most recently watched films, then looks up each film's director through Wikidata.

  • Book Scan · Feed Read

    Scans a public Goodreads shelf feed for the most recently read books.
